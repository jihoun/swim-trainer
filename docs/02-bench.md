# The bench

## Requirements (derived from the ZWIM bench, 35 × 50 × 80 cm)

| Property | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Height | 35–45 cm | Hands must travel through a full pull without hitting the floor |
| Length | 60–80 cm | Supports hips to sternum; shoulders sit just past the front edge |
| Width | 30–50 cm | Narrow enough to allow some body roll, wide enough to be stable |
| Firmness | Firm, slightly forgiving | Stable platform, comfortable chest contact |

Not suitable: Coupang "air track" gymnastics blocks (only ~10 cm thick) and
round/square inflatable ottomans (too short for torso support).

## Setup

- Fold a towel over the front edge under the chest.
- Shoulders just past the edge, head neutral as in freestyle.
- Bands anchored on a door at 90–130 cm. Resistance changes with distance
  from the anchor: further = harder.
- You need one clear straight line of floor (bench + body + arms + band
  length). A hallway works.
